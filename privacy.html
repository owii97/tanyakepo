Privacy Policy

Tanya Kepo Uploader · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how Tanya Kepo Uploader (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Tanya Kepo, handles information when it is connected to a Google account through the YouTube API Services.

Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses the YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the App accesses

  • Channel basic information (channel ID and title) of the authenticated account, used only to confirm that uploads go to the correct channel.
  • Video upload permission, used only to upload the channel owner's own video files with the metadata the owner provides (title, description, tags, category, language and scheduled publish time).

The App does not read, collect or analyze viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics or any data belonging to other users.

How information is stored

  • The OAuth access/refresh token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user.
  • A local log keeps the IDs, titles and publish times of videos the owner uploaded with the App, to prevent duplicate uploads.
  • No information is sent to any server other than Google's APIs. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Stored data is kept only while the App is in use. The owner can delete the local token and log at any time. You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections (security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Revoking access stops the App from using your account; locally stored data is then deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
